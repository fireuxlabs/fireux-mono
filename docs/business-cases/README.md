# FireUX App Business Cases
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

Purpose: map each app's product thesis before it is fully implemented.

These files are internal source material for:
- public `/learn-more` pages
- `app/meta.ts` positioning, SEO, CTAs, and route summaries
- `app/solutions.md` and `/solutions/*` obligation pages

Planning method:
- [Solution Search System](./solution-search-system.md)

Rules:
- Business case describes the intended finished product shape.
- Meta disseminates that product shape to public routes and SEO.
- Solution pages break the product shape into real customer intents.
- Status and roadmap copy carry implementation honesty.
- Tenant apps should not get generic business cases unless they need app-specific strategy.
