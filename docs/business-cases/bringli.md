# Bringli Business Case
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

## Product Thesis
Bringli is the operating system for local producer supply, discovery, orders, and delivery execution.

## Audience Map
- Local producers who need discoverability, buyer trust, and delivery readiness.
- Households that want easier access to local food and goods.
- Businesses that need reliable local supply and dropoff flows.
- Market operators who need supplier onboarding and coverage discipline.
- Delivery operators who need structured drops instead of informal coordination.

## Business Case
Local supply breaks when producer identity, product availability, delivery coverage, orders, and handoff execution live in separate conversations. Bringli creates one structured flow for producer readiness, local discovery, and reliable drop execution.

The first business value is supply-side trust: who produces what, where they deliver, how ready they are, and how customers can confidently order. The marketplace becomes stronger as supplier profiles, delivery zones, products, and orders become structured.

## Engagement Angle
Lead with: local producers, delivered.

Bringli should engage producers and local market partners first, then buyers. The early wedge is supplier readiness and discovery before deep order complexity.

## Meta Dissemination
Bringli meta should focus on local producers, supplier profiles, delivery readiness, coverage areas, buyer discovery, and Drop-powered execution.

## Solution Direction
- Local producer directory
- Supplier profile
- Delivery area setup
- Local product discovery
- Producer onboarding
- Dropoff readiness
- Business supply ordering
- Home delivery flow
- Market expansion playbook
