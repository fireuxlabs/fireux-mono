# BuenoHost Business Case
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

## Product Thesis
BuenoHost creates digital stay guides for properties, guest instructions, check-in, house rules, local recommendations, and host-managed stay information.

## Audience Map
- Short-stay hosts who answer the same guest questions repeatedly.
- Property managers who need consistent guest information across stays.
- Guests who need Wi-Fi, check-in, parking, rules, checkout, and local notes quickly.
- Hospitality teams that need property-aware guide content.
- Sibling FireUX apps that bridge guest guide QR or translation workflows into a property-aware product.

## Business Case
Guest information often lives in messages, PDFs, paper binders, platform descriptions, and host memory. BuenoHost turns that scattered information into a digital stay guide that can be updated, reused, and shared before or during a stay.

The product value is fewer repeated questions, smoother arrivals, clearer house rules, and a more professional guest experience.

## Engagement Angle
Lead with: one digital guide for every stay.

BuenoHost should speak to hosts and property managers who want guest information to be easier to maintain and easier for guests to find.

## Meta Dissemination
BuenoHost meta should focus on digital stay companions, guest guides, check-in instructions, Wi-Fi, house manuals, checkout notes, local recommendations, and property-aware host tools.

## Solution Direction
- Guest guide QR code
- Wi-Fi instructions page
- House manual QR code
- Check-in instructions page
- Checkout instructions page
- Local recommendations guide
- Emergency contact page
- Parking and access guide
- Property digital stay companion
- Host-managed guest information
