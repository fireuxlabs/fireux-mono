# Cleanbox Business Case
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

## Product Thesis
Cleanbox is the operating system for recurring cleaning operations, from scheduling to service proof, quality control, and billing readiness.

## Audience Map
- Cleaning company owners who need predictable recurring service.
- Operations managers who coordinate crews, shifts, locations, and handovers.
- Clients who need proof that work happened and standards were met.
- Crews who need clear assignments and simple service checklists.
- Back-office teams who need invoice-ready service records.

## Business Case
Cleaning operations fail when work is planned in one place, proven in another, and billed from memory. Cleanbox creates a structured flow from service location to job, assignment, QA proof, completion history, and billing readiness.

The product value is operational trust. Every clean should become a verifiable service record: who did it, where it happened, what standard was checked, what proof exists, and what can be billed.

## Engagement Angle
Lead with: schedule, verify, and invoice every clean with confidence.

Cleanbox should speak to recurring cleaning businesses that need reliability more than one-off booking chaos. The buyer wants fewer missed jobs, fewer disputes, clearer crew accountability, and cleaner billing.

## Meta Dissemination
Cleanbox meta should focus on cleaning operations, recurring service, QA proof, crew assignments, compliance, and billing readiness.

## Solution Direction
- Cleaning schedule planner
- Crew assignment workflow
- Cleaning checklist
- Photo proof of service
- Quality assurance sign-off
- Recurring cleaning jobs
- Client-ready service history
- Invoice-ready cleaning records
- Compliance and certification tracking
