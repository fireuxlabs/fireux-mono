# Find My SEO Business Case
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

## Product Thesis
Find My SEO helps businesses find, compare, and contact SEO professionals by problem, sector, service, proof, and fit.

## Audience Map
- Business owners who know they need SEO but do not know what kind.
- Marketing teams that need specialists for technical SEO, SaaS SEO, ecommerce SEO, local SEO, or content strategy.
- SEO professionals who need structured profiles that show proof and specialization.
- Agencies and consultants who want better-fit leads.
- Buyers who want to compare expertise without reading generic sales pages.

## Business Case
SEO discovery is noisy. Buyers struggle to tell who is credible, who fits their sector, and who solves their specific problem. Professionals struggle to present proof in a way buyers can compare.

Find My SEO creates structured discovery for SEO services: professional profiles, categories, sector pages, case studies, proof, availability, and enquiry paths.

## Engagement Angle
Lead with: find the right SEO professional for the problem you actually have.

The product should not try to be a generic directory. Its advantage is problem-led matching and comparable proof.

## Meta Dissemination
Find My SEO meta should focus on SEO professional discovery, comparison, provider proof, specialist categories, buyer intent pages, and lead-ready profiles.

## Solution Direction
- Find an SEO consultant
- Compare SEO professionals
- Technical SEO specialist search
- SEO for SaaS
- SEO for ecommerce
- Local SEO expert discovery
- SEO provider profile
- SEO case study proof
- Lead-ready SEO business profile
- AI search visibility profile
