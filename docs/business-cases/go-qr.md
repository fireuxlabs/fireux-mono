# Go QR Business Case
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

## Product Thesis
Go QR turns one public thing into a scannable QR page or QR code.

## Audience Map
- Small businesses that need public information to be scannable.
- Hospitality teams that need QR menus, guides, notices, and contact actions.
- Venue and event operators who need signs, maps, forms, and event details behind QR codes.
- Local operators who need QR codes without a heavy app.
- Sibling FireUX apps that receive deeper workflows once QR use becomes structured.

## Business Case
Many real-world actions start from a physical object: a sign, table, flyer, room, desk, label, poster, or receipt. Go QR makes those physical entry points useful by turning documents, URLs, contact actions, Wi-Fi, menus, guest guides, and event information into QR destinations.

The product value is speed and clarity. One thing in, one QR code out, with bridges to deeper apps when the workflow grows.

## Engagement Angle
Lead with: turn one public thing into a QR code.

Go QR should stay lightweight. If the customer needs structured menus, translation, guest guides, or property tools, Go QR should bridge to Misebox, Sproochli, or BuenoHost.

## Meta Dissemination
Go QR meta should focus on QR codes, public links, hosted documents, PDFs, URL QR codes, contact actions, Wi-Fi, menus, guides, and bridge flows into sibling apps.
