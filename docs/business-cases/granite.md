# Granite Business Case
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

## Product Thesis
Granite is the operating system for property portfolio control, compliance, documents, incidents, and financial signals.

## Audience Map
- Landlords who need oversight across multiple properties.
- Agencies that manage portfolios for clients.
- Portfolio managers who need consistent records, follow-ups, and accountability.
- Compliance teams who need obligations, evidence, and risk visibility.
- Advisors who need portfolio-level signals without messy spreadsheets.

## Business Case
Property portfolio work becomes fragile when documents, compliance tasks, incidents, client records, and financial signals are scattered. Granite gives portfolio teams a structured branch console for properties, units, clients, evidence, tasks, incidents, and oversight.

The value is control. Every property should have clear records, current obligations, visible issues, and follow-up ownership. Every portfolio should show where attention is needed.

## Engagement Angle
Lead with: rock solid property portfolio control.

Granite should engage people responsible for accountability: agencies, landlords, portfolio managers, and compliance-focused operators.

## Meta Dissemination
Granite meta should focus on portfolio oversight, property control, compliance exposure, document structure, incident follow-up, and financial signals.

## Solution Direction
- Property portfolio dashboard
- Compliance tracker
- Property document vault
- Incident and repair tracking
- Client portfolio management
- Unit and tenancy overview
- Portfolio financial signals
- Audit-ready property records
- Agency branch console
