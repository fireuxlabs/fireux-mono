# Misebox Business Case
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

## Product Thesis
Misebox is the operating system for kitchens, menus, prep, service, suppliers, teams, and hospitality hiring.

## Audience Map
- Kitchen owners and operators who need repeatable service execution.
- Chefs and kitchen managers who need prep, recipes, menus, and handovers to stop living in memory.
- Hospitality groups that need a consistent operating model across locations.
- Suppliers who need clearer product and ingredient connection into real kitchen workflows.
- Hospitality professionals who need profiles that show capability better than a CV.
- Partners who see hospitality operations as fragmented and underserved.

## Business Case
The business case is not another restaurant menu app. Hospitality teams already run complex operations across paper, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, PDFs, supplier messages, staff memory, and half-used back-office tools. Misebox turns that operational mess into structured, reusable kitchen knowledge.

The strongest wedge is public menu and QR menu work. Start with what the business already understands, then grow inward into dishes, allergens, recipes, prep, suppliers, team roles, hiring, and service readiness.

Each operational object reinforces the others. A dish can connect to menu sections, price, ingredients, allergens, recipes, prep tasks, suppliers, translations, and hiring needs. That compounding data model is the defensible product.

## Engagement Angle
Lead with: turn the menu into a working kitchen system.

For operators, the pitch is less drift and fewer repeated explanations. For chefs, it is service-ready kitchen knowledge. For groups, it is a repeatable operating model. For suppliers and professionals, it is connection to real kitchen demand.

For live customer triage, use [Misebox/Sproochli Customer Lanes](./misebox-sproochli-customer-lanes.md) before touching code or data.

## Meta Dissemination
Misebox meta should describe the finished kitchen operating system, not only current scaffolding. It should keep public routes grounded in kitchens, menus, jobs, professionals, suppliers, and console workflows.
