# Sproochli Business Case
Authority: Internal planning
Updated: 2026-05-26

## Product Thesis
Sproochli creates multilingual public pages for instructions, menus, signs, notices, guides, and QR-linked information.

## Audience Map
- Place owners who need visitors to understand important information.
- Hosts and accommodation operators who need guest instructions in multiple languages.
- Restaurants and hospitality teams that need translated public menus or menu information.
- Rental operators who need instructions customers can understand at the point of use.
- FireUX apps that need a language layer for public pages.

## Business Case
Real-world information often sits in one language while the people reading it do not. Sproochli turns public instructions into language-aware pages that can be reached from QR codes, links, signs, menus, guest guides, and notices.

The product value is comprehension at the moment of need. A visitor scans once, chooses the language they need, and reads the right information without downloading an app or asking staff to repeat it.

## Engagement Angle
Lead with: translation-ready public pages for real-world information.

Sproochli should be practical, not generic translation software. It is strongest when tied to physical-world contexts: signs, rentals, guest stays, menus, safety notes, and visitor guides.

For live hospitality triage, use [Misebox/Sproochli Customer Lanes](./misebox-sproochli-customer-lanes.md) before deciding whether Sproochli should stay light or bridge into Misebox.

## Meta Dissemination
Sproochli meta should focus on multilingual QR pages, public information translation, certified page translations, cached generated translations, and language layers for sibling FireUX apps.
