## 1–2. Foundations and responsibility
FireUX extends Nuxt (application framework), Nuxt UI (interface), Firebase (backend) with reusable UX, integration, entity contracts and organization across apps. Native first, explicit dependencies, one authoritative owner per datum. Industry ecosystem extensions sit above reusable foundations; reuse at the highest appropriate level without pushing domain-specific behavior into Core. Commercial objective: rapidly build, maintain and ship apps. Architecture first is a sequencing principle, not an excuse to indefinitely delay products. Long-term ease of building through FireUX is an aspiration, not a claim that Nuxt knowledge is unnecessary today.
There is no settled special central context/module/composable or 'medulla' abstraction. The settled conceptual spine is: configuration connects the App; Firestore describes the App; Nuxt renders the App. Do not invent a central orchestrator to satisfy a metaphor. The three broad pillars discussed are Firebase integration, entities and organizational architecture.

## 3–5. App architecture
One App entity, one foundation, three organizational roles: global (principal ecosystem app), tenant (serves a particular organization in an ecosystem), standalone (independent product). Global and tenant require ecosystem membership; standalone may be inside OR outside an ecosystem. This corrects the recovery request's suggestion that standalone means outside ecosystem participation. No satellite role. Role is just a classification, not a permission, module injection or inheritance mechanism. Ecosystem membership is separate. Hotel Bären is a tenant example; Go QR an independent product example; Food Truck Map is a hypothetical independent product that may participate in Misebox. No special framework per role.
