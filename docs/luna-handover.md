# Luna Piccante Digital Menu Handover

## What Was Built

- Public QR menu pages for Luna Piccante.
- A menu index where guests choose between Hauptkarte and Pizza.
- Original/default menu view that keeps Luna's own menu voice.
- German and English guest-readable menu views.
- French translation-cache output for prototype translation flow.
- Structured menu sections and dish records.
- Kitchen-level currency settings.
- Structured numeric price data with rendered display prices.

## Important Product Note

The digital menu keeps the restaurant's original/default menu voice. For Luna Piccante, the original/default menu is mixed: mostly Italian dish and section names with German descriptions.

German and English are stored as certified Luna menu content. French and Arabic are currently stored as translation-cache output to simulate the future translation system.

## Vegetarian And Vegan Section Note

The Pizza menu has a section currently labelled `Vegetarisch & Vegan` / `Vegetarian & Vegan`. Every pizza item in that section contains mozzarella or another cheese ingredient, so the section is vegetarian but not actually vegan.

Suggested future correction before reprinting or wider promotion:

- Rename the section to `Vegetarisch` / `Vegetarian`, or
- Keep `Vegetarian & Vegan` only if Luna Piccante adds genuinely vegan pizza options or vegan cheese choices.

In the digital menu, this section is styled as a coloured section header like the other pizza category headers, but the wording still needs restaurant approval.

## Arabic Pork Ingredient Clarity

Arabic menu text deliberately names pork ingredients clearly. This is important because pork can be a dietary or religious restriction for many Arabic-speaking guests.

Examples used in the digital Arabic cache:

- Bacon / Speck: `لحم خنزير مقدد`
- Ham / Vorderschinken: `لحم خنزير`
- Parma ham / Parmaschinken: `لحم خنزير بارما`
- Pork steak / Schweinesteak: `ستيك لحم خنزير`
- Pork salami where relevant: `سلامي من لحم الخنزير`

This wording is not a criticism of the menu. It is a guest-safety and clarity choice for translation.

## Not Changed

- Dish ids and slugs were not changed, to keep menu links and data references stable.
- Prices were copied into the digital menu structure, but final price responsibility remains with the restaurant.
- Luna Piccante's menu style, section structure, and dish naming were preserved unless there was a clear typo.
- The physical menu has not been redesigned or reissued as part of this work.
