# FireUX Businesses

Reusable Business identity and Workspace. A Business owns its organisation profile,
optional Place links and Team roster. Its account access is membership-bound and
separate from the roster and from subscription entitlement.

Managed Workspace creation follows App policy and plan capacity. The first tools create a Business, edit its name/description/contact
information, link existing Places and manage Team roster entries. Bookings is an
optional capability supplied by its own package. Interlaken Bike Tours is the
first App registration for these tools.

See [Business context](docs/ai/businesses-context.md) for source owners, migration
and verification. Source migration does not convert live Company records.

Jobs and Supplier selection can also contribute a Business directory identity from a Place. That command grants no Team or operator access.
