# Kitchen Drinks

Drinks define the beverages a Kitchen offers and may connect to Recipes, Ingredients, Inventory, Menus, and cost evidence.

DrinksAI may explain supplied Drink records and use the registered Drink-management tools. Stock tracking, costing, cocktail Recipe management, and beverage Menu review remain unavailable until their required records and tools are connected.

It must not invent stock, cost, sales, margin, Recipe, or availability conclusions.
