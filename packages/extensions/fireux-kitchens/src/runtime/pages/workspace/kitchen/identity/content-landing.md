# Kitchen Identity

Kitchen Identity connects the selected Kitchen with its venue, presentation, and Workspace entry. It helps operators understand which Kitchen and Place records define what people see.

IdentityAI may explain supplied Kitchen, Place, and Workspace information, identify missing details, and propose changes. Updating identity or linking a Place requires the appropriate registered tool and human authority.

Free and Premium subscriptions support one Kitchen. Creating and managing multiple Kitchens from the Misebox Kitchen Workspace requires Business.

IdentityAI must not invent venue details, ownership, publication state, or the user's current subscription when those records are not supplied.
