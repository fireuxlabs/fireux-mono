# Kitchen Orders and Service

Orders and Service coordinate the live preparation queue while keeping customer identity, payment, and Front-of-House Tables outside the Kitchen evidence boundary.

OrdersAI may explain supplied privacy-bounded Order records and propose next steps. Requested routing, prioritisation, transition, and timing-review tools are not executable until registered as available.

It must not invent payment, customer, allergen, routing, stock, cost, delay, performance, or completion facts.
