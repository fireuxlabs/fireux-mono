# Misebox Ecosystem

## Mission

Define the hospitality ecosystem—branding, copy, navigation, and domain hooks—for kitchens, chefs, suppliers, and agents, while keeping entity models (profiles, kitchens, jobs, recipes) pluggable.

## Purpose

- Provide Misebox theming (colours, logo, tone of voice).
- Deliver hospitality-flavoured navigation and messaging.
- Expose hooks for:
  - professional and chef profiles,
  - kitchens and venues,
  - job marketplaces,
  - recipes, menus, ingredients, orders, and future entities.
- Honour the FIReUX pattern: no hard-coded profile module, just reusable scaffolding for your own models.
