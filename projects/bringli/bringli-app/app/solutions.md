# Bringli Solutions

## supplier-profiles

Give local suppliers a clean public surface for who they are and what they provide.

### How this comes to life

1. Create supplier profiles.
2. Add products, coverage, and readiness details.
3. Publish supplier discovery pages.

### Business case

Supplier trust starts with clear identity and capability. Bringli should make local producers discoverable before complex ordering.

### Provisioning

- Supplier records.
- Company/place links.
- Public supplier routes.

### Checklist

- [ ] Confirm supplier profile route.
- [ ] Add coverage/readiness fields.
- [ ] Add public discovery filters.

## delivery-supplier-leads

Create an entry point for buyers, suppliers, and delivery operations to connect.

### How this comes to life

1. Capture buyer or supplier interest.
2. Route the lead to the operator.
3. Connect accepted leads to supplier or delivery workflows.

### Business case

Bringli grows market-by-market. Leads are the lightweight way to test demand before full ordering.

### Provisioning

- Lead/request form.
- Admin review path.
- Supplier and buyer context fields.

### Checklist

- [ ] Add lead form.
- [ ] Add operator review path.
- [ ] Add lead source tracking.

## supplier-network

Use Bringli as the public and operational surface for a connected local supply network.

### How this comes to life

1. Build supplier density in one market.
2. Add coverage and readiness data.
3. Layer order and drop workflows when demand is clear.

### Business case

The network is the durable value: more producers, clearer coverage, better local supply, and reliable delivery execution.

### Provisioning

- Supplier network records.
- Coverage areas.
- Drop/delivery workflow bridge.
