# Cleanbox Solutions

## cleaning-jobs

Create a clear job surface for service work, recurring tasks, and operational follow-up.

### How this comes to life

1. Create service jobs for locations and crews.
2. Assign work and expectations.
3. Track completion and follow-up.

### Business case

Cleaning businesses need predictable recurring service. Jobs should connect schedules, crews, service proof, and billing readiness.

### Provisioning

- Service location records.
- Job records.
- Crew/user links.
- Status and follow-up fields.
