# Richie’s Reinigung

A branded Cleanbox deployment for Richie’s Reinigung — a Swiss cleaning company using FireUX technology to manage daily operations, staff, and clients.

---

## Audience

Richie’s Reinigung management team, on-site crew members, and administrative staff.

---

## Purpose

This project demonstrates how FireUX Cleanbox can be fully customized for a single business.
It keeps the power of Cleanbox but adapts the visuals, copy, and data structure to fit Richie’s Reinigung’s real workflows, team sizes, and client expectations.

---

## Key Features

- Branded UI with Richie’s Reinigung colors and logo
- Crew dashboard for shift assignments and attendance
- QA module with photo uploads and timestamped proof
- Client reports with job summaries and visual verification
- Integrated Stripe billing under Richie’s own account
- Custom compliance section for Swiss safety standards
- Analytics overview for hours worked and job completion rates

---

## Main Pages / Routes

- `/` – company homepage
- `/dashboard` – manager view of daily operations
- `/crews` – active staff list and shift schedules
- `/qa` – photo verification and checklists
- `/clients` – client database and job reports
- `/billing` – invoices and payment records
