# Richie's Reinigung Tenant

## Mission

Deliver the Cleanbox experience tailored for Richie's Reinigung: German-language cleaning services, rota management, and field staff tooling that still honor FIReUX's modular contract.

## Purpose

- Localize the Cleanbox global shell for Richie's markets and service tiers.
- Wire the tenant to the specific service catalogue, pricing rules, and checklist formats Richie's adopts.
- Offer ops teams rota, route, and crew oversight while leaving integrations pluggable.
- Validate that the Cleanbox ecosystem can support multilingual, high-frequency cleaning operations without deviating from FIReUX guardrails.
