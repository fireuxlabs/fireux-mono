# Go QR Solutions

## public-doc-qr-code

Use Go QR when you need one public document to become one scannable public page.

This is the core Go QR flow: upload the public information, give it a short path, and get a link plus a QR code that people can scan in the real world.

### How this comes to life

1. Upload the public document.
2. Choose the short public path people will scan.
3. Go QR hosts the page and creates the QR code.

## url-qr-code

Use this when the destination already exists and you just need a clean QR code for it.

URL QR is for websites, booking pages, forms, social links, product pages, payment links, and any other web destination that already has a URL.

### How this comes to life

1. Paste the URL.
2. Go QR validates the destination and generates the QR.
3. Use the QR on a poster, counter sign, flyer, label, or screen.

## pdf-qr-code

PDF QR is the focused version of public doc QR for menus, notices, terms, forms, guides, and information sheets.

The product promise is simple: upload the PDF, get a public page, then print or share the QR code.

### How this comes to life

1. Upload a PDF.
2. Choose a short public path.
3. Go QR hosts the PDF page and gives you a printable QR code.

## wifi-qr-code

Wi-Fi QR turns network details into a scannable code for guest rooms, counters, offices, events, rentals, and shared spaces.

The goal is to remove typing errors and repeated questions by putting the network join action behind one scan.

### How this comes to life

1. Enter the network name and password.
2. Choose the security type if needed.
3. Go QR creates a Wi-Fi QR code ready for printing.

## phone-call-qr-code

Use a phone call QR when the best next action is simply calling the business, host, venue, or support number.

This is useful on signage, emergency notices, service desks, rentals, flyers, appointment cards, and local business pages.

### How this comes to life

1. Enter the phone number.
2. Go QR creates a tel link QR code.
3. People scan it and their phone prepares the call.

## whatsapp-qr-code

WhatsApp QR is useful for support, bookings, quick questions, group joins, and local business contact.

It is a lightweight way to turn a poster, sign, table card, or leaflet into a direct messaging action.

### How this comes to life

1. Enter the WhatsApp number or invite link.
2. Optionally add a starter message.
3. Go QR creates a code that opens the chat flow.

## map-pin-qr-code

Map pin QR helps people find places that are hard to explain on signs, posters, event pages, and printed directions.

This is useful for hidden venues, temporary event entrances, pickup points, rural stays, parking spots, and meeting points.

### How this comes to life

1. Enter coordinates or a map URL.
2. Choose the preferred map destination where useful.
3. Go QR creates a QR code that opens the location.

## vcard-qr-code

vCard QR is for business cards, reception desks, events, sales teams, service contacts, and public contact sheets.

It lets someone scan once and save the important contact details instead of typing them manually.

### How this comes to life

1. Enter name, phone, email, company, and website.
2. Go QR formats the contact card.
3. People scan it and save the contact on their phone.

## event-info-qr-code

Event info QR gives attendees a simple scan point for schedules, joining notes, venue details, and last-minute updates.

It works for small events, pop-ups, meetings, tours, classes, workshops, and one-off notices.

### How this comes to life

1. Add event title, time, location, and key details.
2. Go QR creates a public event information page.
3. Share the QR on posters, tickets, tables, or screens.

## review-link-qr-code

Review link QR makes it easier to collect Google, Tripadvisor, booking, or platform reviews at the right moment.

The scan should happen where the customer is already happy: at the counter, on a receipt, in a room, at checkout, or after a service.

### How this comes to life

1. Paste the review destination URL.
2. Go QR creates a scannable review link.
3. Place it on receipts, counters, rooms, or follow-up cards.

## feedback-form-qr-code

Feedback form QR is for collecting simple responses from guests, customers, attendees, or staff.

It works with existing forms today and can grow into a Go QR-owned lightweight feedback page later.

### How this comes to life

1. Paste a form URL or create a simple feedback page.
2. Go QR creates a QR code for the form.
3. People scan it and submit feedback on their phone.

## booking-link-qr-code

Booking link QR helps people move from a poster, menu, table card, flyer, or sign into a booking flow.

It is useful when the business already uses a booking system and only needs the physical-world entry point.

### How this comes to life

1. Paste the booking URL.
2. Go QR turns it into a scannable booking code.
3. People scan and continue in the booking system you already use.

## food-menu-qr-code

Go QR can host a menu file today.

Misebox is the next step when the menu needs dishes, sections, prices, allergens, translations, or ordering.

### How this comes to life

1. Upload the menu file if it is already a finished document.
2. Use the generated QR code on tables, windows, flyers, or signs.
3. Move to Misebox when the menu needs to become structured hospitality data.

## guest-guide-qr-code

Go QR can host a guest guide document.

BuenoHost is the deeper app when guest information should become editable, reusable, and property-aware.

### How this comes to life

1. Use Go QR for a simple guest guide document.
2. Use BuenoHost when the guide needs structured check-in, Wi-Fi, rules, recommendations, and host tools.
3. Print the QR where guests naturally need it.

## multilingual-qr-pages

Go QR handles the scannable public page.

Sproochli is the deeper app when that page needs translation and localisation.

### How this comes to life

1. Start with one public Go QR page or document.
2. Move to Sproochli when the information needs language versions.
3. Let visitors scan once and choose the language they need.
