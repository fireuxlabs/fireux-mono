# Tenant Sandbox

## Mission

Be the unbranded, zero-domain tenant used purely to test tenant loading, isolation, and theming, without any opinionated entities or profiles.

## Purpose

- Act as the lab rat for tenant mechanics.
- Remain intentionally plain and disposable.
- Avoid ecosystem features to keep focus on infrastructure.
- Answer the question: "does the tenant system work?" with a clear yes or no.
