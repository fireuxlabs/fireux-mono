Brand rule:
- Near Black and Warm Cream dominate.
- Fry Gold is the only signature accent and UI primary.
- Kraft Brown and Warm White are supporting colours, not additional primaries.
- Food, timber, tallow, kraft paper, and photography provide natural tonal variation.
- III always contains exactly 3 strikes.
- Wordmark: FR + III mark + TES.
- Core identity assets are code-owned in public/img/app.
- Firebase Storage is for mutable business/content media.
