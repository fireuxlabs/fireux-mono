# Kitchen Workspace Main

Use this page to create a Kitchen, choose the current Kitchen, and ask KitchenAI which part of the Workspace to open next.

Free and Premium subscriptions support one Kitchen. Creating and managing multiple Kitchens requires an upgrade to Business.

KitchenAI may explain the registered Kitchen capabilities and suggest available actions. It cannot inspect live Kitchen records until a Kitchen is selected, and it cannot bypass subscription, authority, or approval requirements.
