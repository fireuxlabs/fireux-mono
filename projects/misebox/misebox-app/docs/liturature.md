# Misebox App

The live platform for culinary professionals to build digital profiles, manage recipes, and connect with kitchens, suppliers, and recruiters — powered by FireUX Core.

---

## Audience

Chefs, cooks, recruiters, and hospitality managers across restaurants, hotels, and catering services.

---

## Purpose

The Misebox App is the operational version of the Misebox tenant.
It brings together professional profiles, kitchen tools, and job systems to modernize how the culinary industry manages careers, recipes, and operations — all from one app.

---

## Key Features

- Create and share professional chef profiles
- Verified work history with references and reviews
- Recipe management with cost, scaling, and portion control
- Kitchen prep planning tools for stations and teams
- Supplier integration for inventory and re-ordering
- Job search and temporary placement system
- QR-code portfolio sharing for quick access
- Analytics console for kitchens and professionals

---

## Main Pages / Routes

- `/` – marketing homepage
- `/get-started` – onboard as chef or recruiter
- `/professional` – manage your career profile
- `/jobs` – browse or post open positions
- `/kitchens` – kitchen consoles and tools
- `/recipes` – create and manage recipes
- `/suppliers` – connect to and manage ingredient sources
- `/prep-plans` – organize daily production schedules
