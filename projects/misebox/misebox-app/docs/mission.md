# Misebox App (Global)

## Mission

Be the global entry point for the Misebox ecosystem where hospitality professionals sign up, discover jobs, and trial the platform before upgrading to their own private tenant.

## Purpose

- Act as the front door for Misebox.
- Let chefs and professionals create Misebox profiles.
- Allow agents to post roles and manage pipelines.
- Offer suppliers a future integration surface.
- Encourage upgrades into dedicated tenants (e.g. Niederhorn) or paid tiers by gating advanced features.
