# Kiosk Skin One draft assets

This folder contains source-quality visual drafts for the Misebox-owned Kiosk
Skin One presentation. Drafts are not runtime assets and are not evidence of a
Kitchen's identity, products, menu content, or operational configuration.

`portrait-master-v1.png` is a neutral portrait backdrop master generated for
the reusable skin. Its central column intentionally remains quiet for injected
Kitchen identity, Kitchen- or Dish-owned food photography, and canonical kiosk
controls. It contains no tenant logo, product, copy, icon, or interface.

Before runtime use:

1. review and approve the master;
2. derive the guarded portrait-large, portrait-compact, and portrait-phone
   outputs without adding tenant identity;
3. keep logo, wordmark, food media, copy, prices, and ordering state as
   independent owner-injected layers; and
4. verify legibility, responsive crops, reduced motion, and system-state
   fallbacks in the real Kiosk renderer.

The flattened kiosk moodboard remains design direction only. Do not extract
its fictional content, trademarks, controls, or generated food images as
canonical product assets.
