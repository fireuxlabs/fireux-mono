# Niederhorn

A branded Misebox deployment for Restaurant Niederhorn — a Swiss kitchen using FireUX technology to manage its recipes, prep workflows, and chef training.

---

## Audience

Head chefs, sous chefs, and kitchen managers at Restaurant Niederhorn.

---

## Purpose

This project demonstrates how Misebox can be customized for a real kitchen environment.
It combines recipe management, supplier coordination, and team training in one connected dashboard — tailored specifically to Niederhorn’s daily operations.

---

## Key Features

- Branded interface with Niederhorn logo and theme
- Recipe cards with cost and scaling for each menu item
- Prep plan board organized by kitchen station
- Supplier database with linked ingredient tracking
- Team training modules for onboarding new staff
- Internal notes and feedback system between chefs
- Analytics on ingredient usage and prep time efficiency
- Optional link to Misebox Pro for chef career profiles

---

## Main Pages / Routes

- `/` – kitchen home dashboard
- `/recipes` – list and manage restaurant recipes
- `/prep-plans` – schedule mise en place and daily production
- `/suppliers` – manage ingredient providers and stock levels
- `/team` – chef roster and training modules
- `/analytics` – overview of costs, prep times, and waste

---

## Summary

Niederhorn shows what happens when Misebox goes from platform to plate.
It’s a real restaurant running on FireUX — proof that Misebox can serve not just chefs, but entire kitchens seeking modern, efficient, and data-driven operations.
