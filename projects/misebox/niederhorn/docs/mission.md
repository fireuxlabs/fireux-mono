# Niederhorn Tenant

## Mission

Ship a production-ready Misebox tenant for Berghaus Niederhorn, focused on staffing, ordering, and training so the restaurant can run its operations on shared Misebox infrastructure.

## Purpose

- Deliver a private Misebox instance for one organisation.
- Cover internal staff jobs, shifts, and onboarding.
- Support training materials, SOPs, and menu workflows.
- Provide ordering hooks that help make the business case for adoption.
- Carry Niederhorn branding, domain, and access rules while inheriting Misebox capabilities.
