# Holzworm visual asset record

Ownership: Holzworm tenant presentation assets. Every catalogue image is an
original concept image created for this project with OpenAI built-in image
generation on 2026-07-30. No third-party image, logo, product design, readable
text, or scannable QR code was used as a source or included in the final image.

## Catalogue masthead backdrop

`public/img/app/holzworm/holzrosette-backdrop.jpg` is an original Holzworm
presentation backdrop created with OpenAI built-in image generation on
2026-07-31. The PNG master remains in the Codex-generated-images workspace;
the project uses a 1536 × 1024 px JPEG derivative at quality 84.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-bleed website backdrop for the Holzworm product catalogue
Primary request: one original precision laser-engraved radial rosette on a warm
oak board, made from concentric circles, arcs and measured divisions; it should
feel geometric, not spiritual or bohemian.
Composition: landscape; rosette slightly right of centre; calm wood grain on
the left for foreground UI.
Constraints: no text, logo, people, products, QR-like pattern, watermark, or
third-party design reference.
```

Source / ownership: OpenAI built-in ImageGen original for Holzworm only.
Optimisation: JPEG quality 84, used once as the short catalogue masthead
backdrop; product images remain the primary product presentation.

```text
Use case: product-mockup

Holzworm's wood-laser products together on a pale oak worktable: a dark-walnut
bear key fob, an upright pale-wood Aare line panel, two hexagonal floral

motifs, mystical symbols, bohemian props, tools, or packaging.
```

Source / ownership: OpenAI built-in ImageGen original for Holzworm only.
Optimisation: JPEG quality 84; the image leads the masthead and the original
rosette remains a low-contrast supporting artwork rather than the hero subject.

## Shared generation prompt

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: original ecommerce catalogue card for Holzworm, a Swiss wood-laser atelier
Style/medium: premium photorealistic editorial product photography
Lighting/mood: soft natural workshop or north-window daylight
Materials/textures: believable real wood grain and crisp laser-cut or laser-engraved edges
Constraints: original design only; one clear product subject; no readable text,
logos, brands, watermarks, people, copied designs, or generic stock styling.
```

Restaurant-QR prompts additionally require an empty recessed panel only: no QR
code, barcode, or code-like pattern. Each product row below records the distinct
primary request used with that shared prompt.
